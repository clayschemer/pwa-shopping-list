Category totals reflect only un-checked items (e.g. Produce £1.80 = Bananas + Lime, excluding the checked Tomatoes). Tapping × on a revealed checked row deletes it (same behaviour as for active rows) and it won't reappear on re-toggling. Restoring a checked-off item is still done via shop-mode undo.
